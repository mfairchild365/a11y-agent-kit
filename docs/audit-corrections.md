# Audit corrections log

Maintainer history of corrections to the `accessibility-reviewer` agent's findings. This file is **not** read during an audit and isn't part of the skill. The rules that came out of it live in [`skills/building-accessible-ui/references/audit-calibration.md`](../skills/building-accessible-ui/references/audit-calibration.md), which ships with the skill and is applied on every audit.

## How to add a correction

Tell Claude, in any session: "log a correction: what was logged wrong, and what the right call is". It will:

1. check the correction against the W3C Understanding document for any criterion it touches, and add an entry below that cites the passage,
2. distill it into the right section of `audit-calibration.md` (severity anchors, where to log, or don't log), keeping that file short,
3. add or adjust a seed in `evals/audit_fixture/` and its answer key when the correction can be tested, and
4. commit it on a branch for review.

Entry format: `C-NNN · date · what was logged wrong · the right call · why · where it became a rule`. Newest last. Merge duplicates, and drop an entry once a rule fully covers it.

- **C-006 · 2026-10-07 · the report was noisy and the HTML was hard to open.** A full report (evidence, measurements, stray notes, a duplicate Fix first, an index table) landed in the chat, and `report.html` sat in a temp folder. Right call: the chat gets a short digest with a `Full report:` link, the HTML is rendered by `scripts/render-report.mjs` and shown or opened for the user, nothing sits outside the template's sections, and fields have length caps. Documentation and test-coverage gaps are not findings (one Maintainer notes line under Out of scope). Rule: template (Digest, rules), calibration C. Tested by the digest and noise checks.
- **C-007 · 2026-10-07 · a severity anchor was lowered.** A keyboard scroll-area issue was rated Moderate against the Critical-at-minimum anchor, with the reason that only Safari was exposed. Right call: anchors are floors that can't be lowered. When impact is uncertain, keep the severity and lower the confidence, or move it to Needs verification. Rule: calibration A (applied when the plan was approved on 2026-10-07).
- **C-008 · 2026-10-07 · process cleanup killed about 50 processes by command-line match.** Right call: stop only what you started, by handle; never by name or pattern. Rule: `references/testing.md`, Process safety.
- **C-009 · 2026-10-07 · a border and a panel edge rated Critical under 1.4.11.** The auditor reported a border or panel edge as a Critical 1.4.11 failure. Right call: 1.4.11 doesn't require a boundary when the control has visible text or a sufficiently contrasting icon ("does not require that controls have a visual boundary indicating the hit area", Understanding, Boundaries), and a panel edge isn't a control. It fails only when the element is required to identify the control or its state: an empty input's border, a checkbox square, a meaningful icon without text, a state indicator, or an author-styled focus indicator. Delineating every control is a best practice (same page). Cause: my calibration row lumped 1.4.3 and 1.4.11, and my template example called a panel with no edge in forced colors a 1.4.11 failure. Rule: calibration A and "Not a 1.4.11 failure", `audit-procedures.md` 1.4.11. Tested by the `input-boundary-contrast` seed and the `outlined-button-border` and `panel-shadow-edge` decoys.
- **C-010 · 2026-10-07 · test procedures per criterion.** Added `references/audit-procedures.md` for the error-prone criteria, each drafted from its Understanding document and then checked claim by claim against the raw page text. The procedures are not the scope of an audit.
- **C-011 · 2026-10-07 · Reflow: the 256px case, and a single overflowing line.** Two corrections to the 1.4.10 procedure. (1) The 256 CSS px height applies only to content designed to scroll horizontally, such as text written vertically (traditional Chinese or Japanese). It is not a second test for ordinary pages (Understanding, Intent: "users reading content written in a language where one scrolls horizontally to read ... it is not desired to also need to vertically scroll back-and-forth to read"). (2) The criterion is about being forced to scroll back-and-forth to read line by line. A single line that runs past the edge (one long label or button text) is not a failure, but wrapping it is a best practice; several stacked lines that each overflow do fail. The second point is the maintainer's reading of the Intent, not a sentence on the page, so the procedure marks it "Auditor guidance, confirmed by the maintainer". Rule: `audit-procedures.md` 1.4.10 and calibration A. Tested by the `single-line-overflow` decoy; the `dropdown-reflow` seed (five stacked overflowing names) still fails.
- **C-012 · 2026-10-07 · status messages can't be tested by looking.** The 4.1.3 procedure said to inspect the live region, but a shared announcer adds text and removes it within milliseconds, so a hand inspection sees nothing. Added `scripts/live-region-probe.mjs`, which installs a MutationObserver before the page's scripts and reports each text change with its region, whether the region existed and is exposed, the dwell, and whether focus moved (including inside shadow roots). What it can establish: whether a message is programmatically determinable through a live region. What it can't: whether a screen reader speaks it, or whether a very short dwell is long enough. Rule: a message in no live region or a hidden one fails (High confidence); a present region is Medium; a transient message, a region inserted with its text, or several changes in one task go under Needs verification with the measured dwell, never as a failure, because the Understanding page states no minimum time and no region-must-exist-first rule. Tested by `evals/test_live_probe.mjs` and the `live-region-timing` seed (the "Add student" announcer).
- **C-013 · 2026-10-07 · `list-style: none` logged as a Minor 1.3.1 failure.** The first pasted feedback listed `list-style: none` without `role="list"` as a Minor issue, and I copied it into the fixture as a seed. It isn't a WCAG failure: the markup is still a list, and Safari with VoiceOver drops list semantics from unstyled lists by design (an Apple decision). Right call: don't log it; at most a Best practice. Rule: calibration C. Tested by the `list-style-none` decoy (any report of it fails the checker; add `allowed_tiers: ["best-practice"]` to the decoy to relax it). The fixture now has no Minor seed.
- **C-015 · 2026-10-08 · added a 1.3.1 procedure, covering the whole criterion.** The 1.3.1 entry in `references/audit-procedures.md` covers headings, lists, tables, form groups and labels, emphasized text, emulated links, `role="presentation"` and information shown only by presentation. It was drafted from the Understanding page and the technique pages it lists as failures (F2, F42, F43, F46, F48, F90, F91, F92) and the main sufficient techniques (H42, H43, H48, H51, H63, H71, G141), read from the raw pages. Maintainer decision: a skipped heading level is a Best practice, unless the levels make other headings appear in the wrong place in the outline, which is a 1.3.1 failure (H42 check 1). Not on the page, so marked as guidance: missing landmarks and a missing `h1` are not 1.3.1 failures on their own. The fake-heading severity (Critical) comes from C-014 and is applied in this entry once both branches are merged. Not yet tested by a fixture seed.
- **C-016 · 2026-10-09 · a snackbar's 1.4.11 failure rated Minor.** A non-text contrast failure in a snackbar was rated Minor. Right call: Moderate at minimum. A required cue (a meaningful icon with no text, a state indicator) stays Critical (C-009); any other 1.4.11 failure is never below Moderate. Rule: calibration A. Tested by the `snackbar-icon-contrast` seed.
- **C-017 · 2026-10-09 · 2.2.1 fix advice was incomplete.** The auditor correctly found an auto-dismissing snackbar failing 2.2.1 but suggested only pausing on mouse-in or focus-in. Users may not get the pointer or focus into the message in time. Right call: also say that actionable or important messages must not auto-dismiss, and that dismissed messages can be re-opened (for example a notifications inbox). The Understanding page supports the re-open route: a timed message is acceptable when "there is an alternative that does not rely on a timer", and its toast example relies on the user finding the message elsewhere (the Inbox). The page says nothing about pausing on hover or focus, so that advice is auditor guidance. Rule: calibration A (status messages), `audit-procedures.md` 2.2.1. Tested by the `snackbar-timeout` seed. No severity was given, so it is "by impact" until confirmed.
- **C-018 · 2026-10-09 · status icons treated as decorative.** Snackbar icons (red x, orange triangle, green check) were marked `aria-hidden`, and the auditor didn't report it. They carry the message type, and the text rarely says "Error", "Warning" or "Success". Right call: 1.1.1 failure unless the text states the type; an icon next to text that does state it is decorative (the existing `decorative-icon` decoy stays valid). The Understanding page's decorative rule covers "pure decoration ... providing no information". Rule: calibration A (status messages), `audit-procedures.md` 1.1.1. Tested by the `snackbar-icon-alt` seed and the `snackbar-icon-with-type-text` decoy. No severity was given, so it is "by impact" until confirmed.
- **C-019 · 2026-10-09 · an action inside a live-region toast.** Scott O'Hara ("A toast to an accessible toast", 2019, updated 2025) and Adrian Roselli ("Defining 'Toast' Messages") both advise against interactive controls inside a toast's live region: the control's role isn't announced, hover and focus pausing don't work in browse mode, and keyboard and magnifier users miss it. No success criterion fails on that alone, so the maintainer's call is a Best practice with no severity. The timeout is still 2.2.1 (C-017). Rule: calibration A (status messages). Tested by the `snackbar-undo-in-live-region` decoy. The authoring side is in `components/toast.md`.
- **C-020 · 2026-10-09 · named sections were not looked for.** A `section` given an accessible name while it already has a visible heading produces a redundant announcement for screen reader users. Right call: avoid the name whenever possible, and report it as a Best practice, not a failure. Not a success criterion failure, so there is no severity; a name that tells apart two landmarks of the same kind is needed. Rule: calibration D, `audit-procedures.md` 1.3.1, `references/structure.md`. Tested by the `named-section-with-heading` seed (the dropdown panel).
- **C-021 · 2026-10-09 · inner text changes on a focused button were not looked for.** Changing the inner text of a focused button to show a state change isn't announced by some screen readers. Right call: Best practice, plus a 4.1.3 Needs verification entry when the change is the only way the user learns an outcome. Rule: calibration D, `audit-procedures.md` 4.1.3. Tested by the `button-text-state-change` seed ("Mute alerts" to "Unmute alerts").
- **C-022 · 2026-10-09 · a toast title must be a heading.** A toast or snackbar that has a title marks it up as a heading. A bold `<div>` or `<span>` is a 1.3.1 failure, rated as a fake heading. Rule: calibration A (status messages), `audit-procedures.md` 1.3.1, `components/toast.md`. No fixture seed yet.
